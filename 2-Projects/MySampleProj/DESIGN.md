# Design

Here is your first and final source of truth for everything. What are things called? Semantics are EVERYTHING here. 
Once you start defining definitions (i.e. just names for things) then you must use that definition always. Like coding!

For example, if you were making a Rubix Cube game, and you defined a single rotation a "twist" then ALWAYS when mentioning a rotation, it is a twist.

Also here include your high-level game loop, at the least.
